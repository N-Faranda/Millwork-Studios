What it does

Built for the work that comes back tomorrow.

i.

A line, not a list

Work travels a conveyor: taken in, worked, checked, done. Everyone watches the same belt, and it moves in real time as people touch it.

ii.

Nothing leaves unchecked

Every task passes a quality gate before it closes. The check is a station on the line, not a box somebody remembers to tick.

iii.

Automations that push

Rules that carry work forward on their own — five on Starter, fifteen on Professional, as many as the shop needs on Custom.

iv.

Organizations and products

Shape the work the way the business is actually shaped: an organization, its product lines, the people on each. No folder tree to invent.

v.

Files stay where they live

Sync with Google Drive, OneDrive and iCloud. Plough points at the documents a company already keeps instead of swallowing them.

vi.

On the desk and on the floor

Native desktop apps for macOS and Windows. Mobile joins them from Professional up, for the half of the work that never happens at a desk.
